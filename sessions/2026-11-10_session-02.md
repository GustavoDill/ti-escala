The first upload was served from the CDN cache for the plain download; re-uploading with `cacheControl: '0'` fixed it.
- **Backups** (Windows temp folder): `backup\escala-bucket-before-fix.csv` (pre-fix, corrupted) and the `escala-bucket-backup-*.csv` files written by the helper.

Usage:
```bash
node scripts/convert-escala-xlsx.mjs "<planilha.xlsx>" "<saida.csv>"
```
